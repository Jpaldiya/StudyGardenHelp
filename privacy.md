# StudyGarden Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

StudyGarden is a Discord bot. This policy explains what it stores, why, and how you can have it deleted.

## What we store

When you use StudyGarden, we store only what the bot needs to work:

- **Discord IDs:** your user ID, and the IDs of the servers and channels where you use the bot. We do not store your
  username, avatar, email or messages.
- **Focus sessions:** which plant you chose, how long you focused, start and end times, and whether the plant grew or
  withered.
- **Progress:** seeds, streaks, owned plants and pets, and your chosen theme and pets.
- **Settings:** your time zone, and server settings chosen by server admins (e.g. an announcement channel).
- **Deadlines:** the name you type, the due time, and your reminder choice.
- **Premium status:** whether you have Premium and when it ends. Payments are handled by the payment platform
  (e.g. Ko-fi or Discord). We never see your card or bank details.

StudyGarden does not read message content or listen to voice channels.

## How we use it

Only to run the bot's features: growing your garden, streaks, stats, leaderboards, reminders and Premium perks.
We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything else.

## Where it is stored and for how long

Data is stored in a database on a cloud server that only the bot's developer can access.

- Deadlines are deleted automatically 7 days after they are due.
- Other data is kept while you use the bot, so your garden and progress aren't lost.
- Daily backups are kept for 14 days, then deleted.

## Deleting your data

To have all your data deleted, contact us (below) with your Discord user ID. We will delete it within 30 days;
copies in backups disappear within 14 days after that. You can remove individual deadlines anytime with
`/deadline remove`.

## Children

StudyGarden follows Discord's minimum age requirements. You must meet Discord's minimum age to use it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Continuing to use the bot means you accept the updated policy.
